# Lore and campaign

Gruk returns months after Blackroot, an Orc timber-trading village in Ironwood Forest, was mysteriously burned. Orcs are builders and traders, not inherently villains. Start with a campfire, shelter and basic supplies. Urga is the first recruit. The Goblin clan is ultimately implicated in the destruction; reveal through exploration and relics, not an early exposition dump. Long-term goal: Blackroot Reborn, Great Hall level 5 and population 100+.

Original nine regions: Blackroot, Deep Ironwood, Ashen Peaks, Hunter’s Glade, Goblin Territory, Riverbend, Abandoned Mine, Outer Woods, Bandit Camp. Riverhaven is the tenth region, reached east from Riverbend; preserve original nine-region campaign completion logic. Verify region IDs in Wilderness.ts (Blackroot 4, Riverhaven 9 in archived patches).
